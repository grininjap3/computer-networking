# Task 6 – Ethernet counters and addressing

Late collisions (412) and CRC errors (1,907) with 2,381 receive errors indicate likely duplex mismatch: PC forced to 100 Mbps half duplex while switch is full duplex. Re-enable auto-negotiation on both ends; verify with ethtool or adapter settings and clear/recheck counters. 01:00:5e:0a:01:02 is IPv4 multicast; 3c:52:82 is the OUI prefix of 3c:52:82:4e:11:07. Jumbo MTU 9,000 lowers frame overhead to ~0.64% versus 3.8% and reduces packet rate about 6×, but every device on the storage path must support it. Keep jumbo frames in separate storage VLAN. MRP ring recovery ~200 ms can miss 100 robot commands at 2 ms intervals; HSR/PRP can give zero-time recovery.
