# Task 4 – Transmission media and channel capacity

Choose Cat6A S/FTP for 85 m robot cell near welders (fibre if EMI persists); multimode OM3/OM4 fibre for 140 m office-to-shop-floor run (copper Ethernet limit is 100 m); single-mode fibre for 550 m shop-floor-to-data-centre; carrier/leased single-mode fibre with DWDM/amplifiers for 400 km plant-to-head-office. Shannon capacity at B=250 MHz: 40 dB SNR ≈3,322 Mbps; 15 dB ≈1,257 Mbps. Welding EMI raises bit errors and retransmissions, harming the 10 ms deadline. Wi-Fi capacity may be ample but delay is non-deterministic due to contention, retries and interference.
